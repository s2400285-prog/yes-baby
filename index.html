💌 For My Sweetest Baby Julie

My baby Julie, my love, my sweetest girl... ♡

I hope whenever you read these words, you can feel how softly and deeply you are held in my thoughts. You are so special to me, baby. In a world full of noise, finding you has given my heart a place of peace, warmth, and pure joy.

Thank you from the bottom of my heart for your time, your kindness, your gentleness, and all the quiet, sweet things you do every day. The way you listen, the sound of your laugh, your sleepy voice in the morning, and the simple moments we share — they might seem ordinary to the world, but to me, they are the most meaningful memories I own.

I want you to always know that with me, you are safe. You do not ever have to pretend to be strong or perfect to be loved by me. I love the real you, baby. I want to keep learning about you, understanding your feelings, supporting every dream God places in your soul, and respecting your beautiful individuality through every season of life.

"Love is patient, love is kind. It does not envy, it does not boast, it is not proud. It does not dishonor others, it is not self-seeking, it is not easily angered, it keeps no record of wrongs. Love does not delight in evil but rejoices with the truth. It always protects, always trusts, always hopes, always perseveres."

1 Corinthians 13:4–7

I thank God every single day for letting our paths cross. I pray that under His faithful guidance, what we are building together will grow through patience, gentle kindness, quick forgiveness, and unconditional grace.

I promise to keep choosing you, appreciating you, respecting your heart, and making an honest, devoted effort to love you better with each passing sunrise.

"My baby Julie, when I asked if I could court you and you said YES, I never knew how many beautiful feelings and memories could begin with that one little answer. But I am so thankful that I took that chance and that you gave me yours. I want to keep knowing you, keep making you smile, keep praying for you, and keep showing you that my intentions are sincere. I cannot promise that every day will be perfect, but I can promise to keep trying, to listen, to understand, and to treat your heart with care. If God allows us to share many more days together, I hope I can keep making you feel special in the little ways that matter most. I love you so much, my Baby Julie, and I want to keep choosing you with a sincere heart, today and in all the days ahead. ♡"

Forever yours with all my devotion,
Your boy who will always cherish you ♡
